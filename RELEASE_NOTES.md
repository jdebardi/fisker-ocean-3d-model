First public release of the controllable Fisker Ocean 3D model.

- Standard and lightweight GLBs, editable Blender source and a ready-to-run interactive preview.
- Custom/catalogue paint, six wheel presets, steering, wheel rotation and car yaw.
- Independent front/tail/high/brake/reverse lighting, synchronised indicators and optional soft headlight spill.
- Four framed opening doors, fixed windscreen/quarter panes, retractable handles, opening hatch and lift-and-slide solar roof.
- Shared solar gradient across the moving/fixed roof sections.
- Generic LLM instructions and integration guides for web, Android, Unity, Unreal, Apple platforms, Flutter/React Native and Qt.
- CC BY 4.0 model assets/documentation and MIT project software, with original-creator attribution.

**Getting started:** download and extract the complete ZIP, then follow README.md. The MP4 is a quick 30-second demonstration. Individual GLBs are also attached for integrations; the ZIP includes their binding metadata, source scene, licence files and documentation.

The release version is **3.0.0**; the compatible runtime contract remains **2.0**. Both GLBs are checked with the Khronos validator. Native engine adapters and target-device benchmarks are not supplied. Direct file:// preview startup is documented but was not browser-verified by the preparation tooling; the served preview and local GLB selection were exercised.

Based on [Fisker Ocean (low-poly) by LagzDesign](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/fisker-ocean-low-poly-f506bfe876864c04b68bdfc59070739a), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). This is an unofficial modified model; see ATTRIBUTION.md and LICENSE.md.
