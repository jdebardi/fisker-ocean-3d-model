# Sources and visual accuracy

## Model

[Fisker Ocean (low-poly), LagzDesign](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/fisker-ocean-low-poly-f506bfe876864c04b68bdfc59070739a), CC BY 4.0. The user supplied the downloaded Blender source. The runtime version includes repaired/smoothed rear geometry and separately controllable wheels and lamps.

## Wheel and paint references

[Fisker Ocean Product Guide, February 2024, version 11](https://downloads.ctfassets.net/cghen8gr8e1n/4YChy5ujfeqDpAHOUKkcVp/baac2c6104820ff3723323fac73dba35/Fisker_Ocean_Product_Guide_Feb_2024_US_V11.pdf), printed pages 21–23 and 28–29.

The guide describes three-spoke SlipStream, seven-spoke AirGlider, ten-spoke Vortex and the AeroStealth cover. F3a/F3b and F5a/F5b are finish variants of their respective geometry families. The meshes here reproduce those broad visual designs with lightweight procedural geometry; fine machining, carbon-flake appearance, manufacturing dimensions and every surface detail are not claimed to be exact.

Paint names and finish categories come from the catalogue. Hex colours and PBR values are artistic display approximations. Marine Layer and Solar Orange were marked Available 2024 in the reference; actual delivery is not verified. The preview labels them as announced finishes. Catalogue inclusion alone is not proof that every listed colour was delivered in every market or trim.

The complete manufacturer PDF is not redistributed in the kit. Use the source link for reference illustrations.
